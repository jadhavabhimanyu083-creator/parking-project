Help & College Viva Guide

Quick reference guide and questions commonly asked by college examiners.

Q1: How does the system allocate slots automatically?

The system models the parking plot as a Graph of accessible cells. When a vehicle arrives, BFS (Breadth-First Search) computes the shortest path distance from the Entrance cell to all available slots compatible with the vehicle type (Car or Bike). It then greedily assigns the slot with the minimum BFS distance in O(V + E) time.

Q2: What happens when the parking lot is completely full?

Vehicles are automatically enqueued into a dual Waiting Queue Manager. Emergency / VIP vehicles are inserted into a Priority Queue, while regular vehicles enter a FIFO queue. Whenever an active vehicle checks out and frees a slot, the head vehicle is automatically dequeued and allocated the freed slot!

Q3: How are duplicate entries and double allocations prevented?

The C++ backend maintains an unordered_map (Hash Table) of active vehicle plates. If a vehicle plate is already active, the entry attempt is rejected immediately with an explanatory error. Slot status flags are locked via Windows CRITICAL_SECTION mutexes ensuring thread safety and preventing race conditions.

Quick Tutorial: Creating a Custom Plot

Welcome to the ParkingFlow Plot Designer. Follow these 3 easy steps:

  1. Basic Details: Enter the name of your facility, location, and choose Car, Two-Wheeler, or Mixed layout.
  2. Layout Design: Set your rows and columns. Use the Palette tools to place Driving Aisles, Entry gates, Exit gates, and EV charging facilities. Made a mistake? Click Undo to revert using the LIFO Stack!
  3. Preview & Pricing: Set your custom hourly tariff rates and click Save. Your plot is immediately saved to disk and ready for real-time parking!
